Where it all begins

A Neighborhood Tradition

North Star Bakery is built around a simple idea: good food has a way of bringing people together. Whether someone stops in for a loaf of bread, picks up pastries on the way to work, or orders a cake for a family celebration, we want every visit to feel welcoming.

We believe the best bakery experiences come from attention to detail, familiar flavors, and the care that goes into preparing something by hand.
